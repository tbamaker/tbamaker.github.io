Screenshots taken from the reader itself, in English. Book covers are hidden.

What is inside

    Same reader underneath. EPUB and text files, bookmarks, a dictionary and Wi-Fi transfer: that part is CrossPoint Reader, an open project this work is built on.

    Install it

    1. Plug in. Connect the X3 to a computer with a USB-C cable that carries data, and wake it up.
    2. Open the install page in Chrome or Edge and pick this edition and your language.
    3. Press Install and choose the reader's USB port. Wait about two minutes without unplugging.
    4. Say hello. The reader restarts on its own. Your books on the card are not touched.
    Before you install. A hobby project with no warranty: you install it at your own risk. You can always go back to CrossPoint Reader or the original firmware with the same cable.
