Install

It installs from this page, with nothing to download. You need a computer with Chrome or Edge and a USB-C cable that carries data.

Not open yet. The editions are still being tested, so the install button is off for now. This page already works the way it will: pick your reader, your edition and your language.

How it goes

  • Plug in and wake up. Connect the reader to the computer and make sure its screen is on.
  • Pick the port. In the browser dialog choose the reader's USB port, usually "USB JTAG/serial debug unit".
  • Wait two minutes. Do not unplug while it writes. The reader restarts on its own.
  • Say hello. On first start you choose your companion and give it a name.
Your books stay. Installing does not touch the memory card. It only replaces the reader's software.
Before you install. This is a hobby project with no warranty: you install it at your own risk. Some units sold on AliExpress have USB flashing locked and cannot be flashed at all.

Questions

Can I go back?

Yes. Reinstall CrossPoint Reader from its website, or the original Xteink firmware, with the same cable.

Can I switch edition later?

Yes, install another one from this page. Editions share your reading progress, so you do not start again.

Do I need Wi-Fi?

No. It is only used for the weather and to set the clock, once a day, and only on a network you already saved.

Why is the X4 marked "coming soon"?

The X4 has a different screen size and no clock chip, so every screen has to be checked on a real one first.
