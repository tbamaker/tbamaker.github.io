Make your e‑reader yours

InkMaker OS is free firmware for Xteink e-readers, in editions with very different personalities. Pick the one that sounds like you, install it from your browser, and your reader stops being a grey rectangle.

The editions

Same reader underneath, six different worlds on top. They are all in the workshop right now: none can be installed yet.

What every edition does

Underneath, it is still a complete reader: EPUB and text files, bookmarks, a dictionary, Wi-Fi transfer. That part is CrossPoint Reader, an open project this work is built on.

On top, each edition gives your reading a shape. Something that grows when you read and notices when you do not. Milestones, a daily goal you set yourself, a sleep screen worth looking at.

  • Free and open. No account, no ads, no tracking. The source code is public.
  • Your books stay. Installing only replaces the reader's software. The memory card is not touched.
  • Reversible. You can go back to the previous firmware with the same cable.
  • English and Italian. Each edition comes in both.

Who makes this

One person and an AI. I bring the ideas, the testing and a 3D printer; Claude writes the code with me. I think that is worth saying openly.

Most of my time goes into 3D printing: things for my desk and add-ons for e-ink readers, like stands, cases and organizers. I am still learning, which is why the page is called Trying to be a Maker. The files are free on MakerWorld.
