How to use them

  1. Copy. Put the .bmp files you like in a folder called sleep at the top of the memory card. Use the Wi-Fi transfer or a card reader.
  2. Switch on. On the reader: Settings → Display → Sleep Screen → Custom.
  3. Sleep. Every time the reader goes to sleep it picks one at random.
  • Want only one? Name it sleep.bmp and put it at the top of the card: it always wins over the folder.
  • Which firmware? Works on CrossPoint Reader and on every InkMaker OS edition. Other firmware may use a different folder.
  • Why BMP? These are 2-bit files with the reader's own four greys: they show exactly as designed, and each one is about 100 KB.
  • The PNG is the same picture, for your phone or for sharing.
